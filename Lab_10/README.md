# Flowers

**Author:** Gauhar Zhanuzak

**Theme:** Flowers and Their Meanings

## About the App

This app is dedicated to exploring different types of flowers, their names, meanings, and popularity around the world. Users can discover interesting facts about flowers, learn where they are most popular, and explore their symbolism in different cultures.

## Main Idea

The goal of the app is to help users discover the beauty and diversity of flowers through pictures and interesting information.
